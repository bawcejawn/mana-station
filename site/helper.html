Your pass, everywhere

The free Mana Station helper lets your pass pay your transactions on any Koinos app. You keep using your wallet exactly as before.

What it does

  1. An app asks you to sign

    Any Koinos site that asks your wallet to approve a transaction.

  2. The helper asks the station

    "Would you pay for this?" If your pass covers it, the helper sets the station as the payer.

  3. You approve as usual

    Your wallet shows the transaction and you approve it. If the pass doesn't cover it, nothing changes and your own Mana pays.

  • It holds no keys and can't sign anything.
  • It never changes what a transaction does, only who pays the Mana.
  • It leaves alone anything another sponsor already pays for, such as Kondor's free mana.
  • It's free, open source and small enough to read in a few minutes.

What it sends, and to whom: see the privacy notice. Using it means you accept the terms. Always check each transaction in your wallet before approving it.

On a phone

Most phone browsers can't add extensions (Chrome and Safari on phones, for example), so the helper is for computers: Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chrome-based browsers. On your phone, your pass still works in every app that supports Mana Station: those apps ask the station first and it covers you automatically, with nothing to install.

Install it on a computer (testnet version)

Works in Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Opera and other Chrome-based browsers. Once published it's one click from the Chrome Web Store (also used by Brave and Opera) or Microsoft Edge Add-ons.

The helper isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Until it is, load it yourself in Chrome. It's the "Mana Station helper" folder next to these pages.

  1. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose the Mana Station helper (testnet) folder.
  3. Reload this page. The check below should say the helper is on.

Chrome warns that it can "read and change data on all websites": it has to run on any Koinos site to set the payer there. It does nothing else.

Check that it works

Kondor's current version only knows Koinos mainnet and an older testnet, so on this playground a stand-in wallet built into this page plays Kondor's part: it receives the signing request exactly as Kondor would, after the helper has seen it, and signs with your test wallet (top right). On mainnet it's Kondor itself.
