Help

Every Koinos transaction needs Mana, and Mana comes from holding KOIN. Mana Station holds the KOIN and pays the Mana, so you, your users or your apps don't have to.

A Mana pass for your wallet

  1. Buy a pass

    On Get Mana, pick a daily amount and 1, 7 or 30 days. Your wallet doesn't need KOIN: the station pays for the purchase too.

  2. Use apps as usual

    With the free pass helper in your browser, your pass pays on any app, up to your daily amount. Apps that work with Mana Station do it even without the helper.

  3. Cancel any time

    Whole days you haven't started come back to you in tUSDT (vUSDT or vUSDC on mainnet).

To have your pass used automatically on any app, add the free pass helper to your browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chrome-based browsers on a computer). On a phone, your pass works in every app that supports Mana Station, with nothing to install.

A pass never pays to move tokens (sending, approving or burning KOIN or stablecoins), never pays for uploading a contract, and only pays for transactions your own wallet signs.

Covering your app's users

  1. Put your app on chain

    Not uploaded yet? Get Mana uploads it with a 24-hour ticket and refunds the unused part.

  2. Buy an app plan

    A daily amount shared by all its users. Your app's own key co-signs, so nobody can buy a plan for an app that isn't theirs.

  3. Point your app at the station

    Send users' transactions with the station as payer and the user as payee. If the station says no, fall back to the user's own Mana.

For developers. With Koilib: { payer: STATION, payee: user, rcLimit: "19000000" }. Keep rcLimit close to what the action really uses: the station counts the limit, not what was spent.

Free Mana for the owner's apps

On the Owner page, the station's owner adds any contract with a size (Small, Medium, Large or a custom amount a day) and changes or removes it in one step. Users of those apps pay nothing and the owner pays no fee.

Questions

What happens if I cancel?

You get back every whole day you haven't started. The current day counts as used, so one day is the minimum charge. It keeps working until the end of that day.

Bought 7 days, cancelled on day 1: 6/7 of the price back.
How do upload tickets work?

A ticket reserves Mana for 24 hours at the full price, whether you use it or not. End it early and you get back the value of the Mana you didn't use, reduced by the time that has passed, with at least 5% kept.

Can one user use up an app's whole plan?

No. Each user has a few actions a minute and a daily cap, and brand-new accounts get a smaller share on their first day.

How many purchases can one wallet hold?

Up to five at a time: passes, app plans and upload tickets together.

What if the station is paused?

It pays for nothing while paused, and the paused time is added back to your term.

Who holds my payment?

The station contract holds it until your term ends, then it goes to the operator, or back to you if you cancel. The station's own key can't move it. Anything else that moves money or changes the code needs two of the three council members, then a 48-hour public wait, during which any one of them can veto. It isn't fully trustless, but no single person can take your payment.

I got "the station didn't cover this one"

You hit a limit for now: a few actions a minute, your daily amount, or the station's daily budget. Wait a minute and try again. A refused attempt costs nothing.
