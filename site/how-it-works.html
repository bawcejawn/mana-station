From your wallet to free play in five steps.

This guide is for people who build Koinos apps. If you just want to play, skip to the part for players.

Taking part as a builder

You buy a daily amount of Mana for your app. Every user of your app then draws from it when they play.

The builder dashboard opens with mainnet. Until then, this is exactly what it will ask of you.

  1. Connect your wallet

    Use Kondor or any Koinos wallet that holds a little vUSDT or vUSDC. You don't need KOIN.

  2. Choose your app and a daily amount

    Enter your app's contract address, how much Mana a day your players may use, and a term of 1, 7 or 30 days. You'll see the exact price before anything is charged.

  3. Approve with your app's key

    Your app's own key signs the purchase too. That proves the app is yours, so nobody else can claim it.

  4. Pay in vUSDT or vUSDC

    One transaction approves the amount and pays it. The payment is held until your term ends, so you can still cancel.

  5. Point your app at the station

    Set the station as the payer on your users' transactions. Your players sign as usual; the station covers the Mana.

    For developers: with Koilib, pass payer: STATION and payee: user in the transaction options. If free play is busy, fall back to the user's own Mana.

Pricing

Introductory prices, per 1,000 Mana a day. You pay once for the whole term.

Big uploads, such as putting a whole contract on-chain, cost $0.05 per 1,000 Mana, with a minimum of 5,000 Mana. That Mana is reserved for you for 24 hours.

No auto-renew. Prices may rise for new purchases, never for a term you've already paid for.

Good to know

The rules the station follows, in plain words. These match the draft terms, which are still under legal review.

Which transactions does my allowance cover?

Any transaction where every step calls your app's contract. A transaction that also calls another contract, or moves tokens directly, isn't covered.

What if I cancel?

You get back every whole day you haven't started, in the stablecoin you paid with. The current day counts as used, so the minimum charge is one day.

Bought 7 days and cancelled on day 1: you get 6/7 of the price back.
Can one player use up my whole allowance?

No. Each player has a few actions a minute and a daily cap, and brand-new accounts get a smaller share on their first day. That keeps bots from draining what you paid for.

How many purchases can one wallet hold?

Up to five at a time, counting daily allowances and big uploads together.

How do refunds work for big uploads?

The Mana is reserved for you for 24 hours at the full price, whether you use it or not. If you end early, you get back the value of the Mana you didn't use, reduced by the time that has passed. At least 5% is always kept.

Half the Mana unused, with 18 of 24 hours left: 50% × 75% = 37.5% of the price back.
What if the station is paused?

Paused time is added back to your term, so you don't lose days you couldn't use.

Who holds my payment?

The station contract holds it until your term ends. Then it goes to the operator, or back to you if you cancel. The station's own key can't move it on its own.

Anything else that moves money or changes the station's code needs two of the three council members to approve, then a 48-hour public wait. During the wait, any one member can veto. It isn't fully trustless, but no single person can take your payment.

Just here to play?

There's nothing to set up. If an app uses Mana Station, your actions in it are free. Sign with your wallet as usual.

If free play is busy, the app may ask whether you want to use your own Mana instead. A refused attempt never costs you anything.
