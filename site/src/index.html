Koinos transactions, already paid for.

Every Koinos transaction needs Mana, and Mana comes from holding KOIN. Mana Station holds the KOIN and pays the Mana for you. You pay a small daily amount in stablecoins, or nothing at all when an app covers you.

Get Mana Try it free

What do you need?

  • Mana for my wallet A daily Mana pass. The station pays your own transactions on any Koinos app, so you never run out. It never pays to move tokens. Get a pass
  • Mana for my app Your users act for free. Buy a daily amount for your app's contract and every user draws from it, with fair limits so no one can drain it. Cover my app
  • Free Mana for my own apps For the station's owner: give any of your contracts a free daily amount, pick a size, change or remove it in one step. Open the owner page

How it works

  1. Pick a daily amount

    For your wallet or for your app, for 1, 7 or 30 days. You see the exact price first.

  2. Pay in stablecoins

    vUSDT or vUSDC. No KOIN needed: the station even pays the Mana for the purchase itself.

  3. Transactions just go through

    Your transactions name the station as the payer, and the station pays, up to your daily amount. Cancel any time for the unused days.
