What is Koinos Mana?

Koinos is a blockchain with no gas fees. Instead, every transaction uses Mana. Here's what that means for you, in two minutes.

Mana is the energy bar of your wallet

Every Koinos wallet has Mana, and the amount comes from the KOIN it holds: each KOIN can carry one Mana. Hold 10 KOIN and you have up to 10 Mana. Each transaction you send spends a little, and bigger transactions spend more.

Mana isn't a token. You can't buy it on an exchange or send it to someone. It simply refills by itself: 20% of your total every day, so an empty wallet is full again within five days.

Think of it like a game's energy bar: you spend it by playing, and it recharges while you wait.

Why "no gas fees" still needs Mana

On most blockchains, every transaction costs a fee in the chain's coin, and that coin is gone once spent. On Koinos, using the network costs Mana instead, and Mana comes back. Your KOIN stays yours: spending Mana only locks a matching bit of KOIN until the Mana refills.

That's great once you hold KOIN. The catch: a brand-new wallet with no KOIN has no Mana, so it can't send anything at all, not even its first transaction. And busy wallets and apps can run their Mana down faster than it refills.

Someone else can pay your Mana

Koinos lets a transaction name a payer: another account that spends its own Mana for you. You still sign and approve your own transaction; the payer only covers what it costs. This is how apps let new users start without buying KOIN first, and it's what makes free, gasless transactions possible on Koinos.

Where Mana Station comes in

Mana Station is a payer anyone can use. It holds a large tank of KOIN, so it has lots of Mana, and it pays for transactions that its clear rules accept:

  • A Mana pass pays your own wallet's transactions on any Koinos app, up to a daily amount, from $0.03 a day.
  • An app plan pays the transactions your app's users make with your app, so they play free, even with an empty wallet.
  • Free Mana the station's owner gives to chosen apps.

You pay in dollar stablecoins, never KOIN. The station never holds your keys, and it never pays to move tokens, only for using apps.

Quick answers

How much Mana does a transaction use?

It depends on what the transaction does. A simple app action uses roughly 0.16 Mana; uploading a contract or posting a lot of text uses more.

What happens when my Mana runs out?

You can't send transactions until it refills (20% of your total a day), unless someone else pays, such as an app or a Mana Station pass.

Can I send all of my KOIN?

Not quite all: the transfer itself needs a little Mana, so send about 99%, or have a payer cover the transfer.

Is Mana the same as gas?

It plays the same role, paying for the network's work, but Mana isn't spent forever: it refills, and your KOIN stays in your wallet.
