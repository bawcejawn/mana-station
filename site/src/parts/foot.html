Prepaid transaction-fee credit: not an investment, not money and not transferable. Payments are held by the station contract until a term ends; cancel any time for the unused part. The station's key alone can't move funds: that takes two of three council members, a public wait and no veto. Smart contracts and blockchains carry risk; always check transactions in your wallet.
