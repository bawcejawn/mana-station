Privacy notice

Draft for legal review, version 2026-10-21.

Short version: no accounts, no tracking, no ads. What you do on a blockchain is public by design.

The website

  • It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.
  • To show the station and send your transactions, your browser talks directly to a public Koinos node (on mainnet, api.koinos.io). That node sees your IP address and the requests, like any website you visit.
  • The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its blockchain library from the jsDelivr CDN, which see your IP address when you load the page.
  • Settings such as the wallet you picked are kept in your browser's own storage and never sent to us. On the testnet playground, test wallets are kept the same way; they hold nothing of value.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

The station (blockchain)

Purchases, refunds and the transactions the station pays for are recorded on the Koinos blockchain, which is public and permanent: anyone can see the wallet addresses, amounts and times involved. We can't change or delete that data. The station keeps per-wallet usage counts on chain to apply its limits.

The browser helper

  • When a site asks your wallet to sign a transaction, the helper sends one read-only question to a public Koinos node: your wallet address and that transaction's operations (which contracts and functions it calls, with their arguments). The node answers whether the station would pay. Nothing is sent to us.
  • It stores only its on/off setting and a count of transactions covered today, in your browser.
  • It never reads your keys, passwords, page contents or browsing history, and never sends anything anywhere else.
  • It needs permission to run on all websites so it can act on any Koinos app you use. Its code is open source.
