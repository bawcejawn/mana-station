Let your users play free.

Mana Station pays the Koinos transaction cost for your app's users. You prepay a daily amount of Mana in stablecoins, and your players never need KOIN to get started.

Who it's for

Every Koinos transaction costs Mana, which comes from holding KOIN. New players usually have none. The station covers it for them.

App builders

You pick how much your players can do each day

  • Buy a daily Mana allowance for your app's contract, for 1, 7 or 30 days.
  • Pay in vUSDT or vUSDC. No KOIN to buy, lock up or manage.
  • Every player of your app is covered, with built-in limits so one account can't use it all.
  • Cancel any time and get back the whole days you haven't used.

Players

You just play

  • Nothing to install or set up beyond your usual wallet.
  • If an app uses Mana Station, your actions in it cost you nothing.
  • If free play is busy, the app can offer to use your own Mana instead. Refused attempts never cost you anything.
