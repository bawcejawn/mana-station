How it works

Koinos has no gas fees. Instead, every transaction uses Mana, which comes from holding KOIN. Mana Station holds the KOIN, so you don't have to.

  1. Pick a daily amount

    For your wallet or your app, for 1, 7 or 30 days. You see the exact price before you pay.

  2. Pay with stablecoins

    vUSDT or vUSDC. Your wallet doesn't need any KOIN or Mana: the station even pays for the purchase itself.

  3. Transactions just go through

    The station pays the Mana, up to your daily amount. Your wallet still asks you to approve each one, as always.

Questions people ask

What is Mana on Koinos?

Mana is what a Koinos transaction costs, instead of a gas fee. Every wallet gets Mana from the KOIN it holds, and Mana refills by itself, about 20% a day. A wallet with no KOIN has no Mana, so it can't send anything on its own. Read the full guide to Koinos Mana.

Do I need KOIN to use Mana Station?

No. You pay for a pass or app plan in a dollar stablecoin (vUSDT or vUSDC), and the station pays the Mana for that purchase too, so even a brand-new, empty wallet can start.

How much does it cost?

From $0.03 for 1,000 Mana a day for one day, $0.20 for seven days or $0.75 for thirty. 1,000 Mana covers about 6,000 simple actions.

Can my app's users make free, gasless transactions?

Yes. Buy an app plan for your contract and the station pays your users' transactions with your app, with fair per-user limits so nobody can drain it. If your plan ends, your app keeps working: users simply pay with their own Mana again.

Does it work with my wallet?

On a computer, the free pass helper for Chrome, Brave and Edge sets the station as the payer before your wallet asks you to approve. Apps that support Mana Station do this by themselves, in any wallet and browser.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in every app that supports Mana Station: those apps ask the station first, with nothing to install. Phone browsers can't add extensions, so the pass helper is for computers.

Is it safe? Who holds my money?

The station never holds your keys and can only pay for transactions, never change them. Your payment is held by the station contract until your term ends. Moving funds or changing the code takes two of three council members and a public waiting period. Cancel any time and get back every unused day.

What happens when my pass ends?

Nothing breaks. There's no auto-renew; your transactions are simply paid with your own Mana again until you renew.
