Buy Mana for your app

This is the builder's side of the station, running on the Koinos testnet with a test stablecoin (tUSDT). Nothing here costs real money.

Big upload ticket

A one-off chunk of Mana, reserved for you for 24 hours. Use it to put your app's contract on-chain, or for one large transaction. End it early for a partial refund.

Daily free play

Ongoing access: a daily amount of Mana for your app's users, for 1, 7 or 30 days. Every player draws from it. Cancel for the whole days you haven't started.

Your wallets

Two wallets take part. The buyer pays in tUSDT and needs a little Mana of its own to send the purchase. The app wallet is your app: on Koinos a contract lives at the address of its key. It never needs any KOIN.
